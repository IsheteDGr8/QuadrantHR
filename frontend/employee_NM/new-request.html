Enterprise Ticketing & Routing

Select your submission classification below for direct routing to specialized operational teams.

Standard Support Ticket

Logged under your Azure Entra ID profile for direct department tracking.

When to use this request: Use this form for general IT support, software/hardware access issues, account management, HR operational questions, or administrative requests linked to your active profile.

Leave Management Request

When to use this request: Use this form to schedule Paid Time Off (PTO), sick leave, parental leave, or bereavement leave. Once approved, dates automatically sync with your team calendar and notify your handover lead.

Note: This ticket will cycle through upper management for approval beforehand.

Confidential / Anonymous Submission

Your Entra ID credentials and user profile are stripped from this ticket before reaching management review queues.

When to use this request: Use this form for secure workplace feedback, ethics or compliance reporting, or sensitive suggestions where you require total anonymity from department management views.
Talking to an Agent Tips
  • Include clear error codes or screenshots in your active chat thread.
  • Be specific about your hardware or software version numbers.
  • Keep your ticket reference ID handy for quick follow-ups.
Genie AI Quick Routing Notice

Submitting tickets through these forms instantly generates a live ticket thread. You can also use the "Ask Genie" assistant widget in the bottom-right corner for instant answers before a human agent picks up your case.
