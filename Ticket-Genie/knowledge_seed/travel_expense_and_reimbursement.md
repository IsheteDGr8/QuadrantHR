# Business Travel, Expense, and Reimbursement Policy

Owner: Finance | Effective: January 1, 2026

Business travel requires manager approval before booking. Employees must use the approved travel platform and select reasonable economy airfare and standard lodging within published city limits. Premium travel requires documented executive approval. Personal extensions are allowed only when they do not increase company cost or risk.

Itemized receipts are required for expenses of $25 or more and for all lodging. Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days after travel. Reasonable meals, ground transportation, baggage, required connectivity, and approved customer expenses are reimbursable. Alcohol, upgrades, minibar items, traffic fines, personal entertainment, and expenses for companions are not reimbursable unless a documented business exception applies.

Managers verify business purpose and policy compliance; Finance performs final audit. Employees must return accidental overpayments promptly and must never divide transactions to avoid approval thresholds.
