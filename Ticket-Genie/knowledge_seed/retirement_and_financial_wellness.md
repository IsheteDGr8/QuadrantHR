# Retirement and Financial Wellness Policy

Owner: Total Rewards | Effective: January 1, 2026

Eligible employees may participate in the company 401(k) plan beginning with the first payroll after 30 days of service. Employees are automatically enrolled at a 4% pre-tax contribution unless they elect a different percentage or opt out. The company matches 100% of the first 3% of eligible compensation contributed and 50% of the next 2%. Employee contributions are always fully vested; employer matching contributions vest 25% after each completed year and are fully vested after four years.

Employees may choose pre-tax and Roth contributions, subject to plan and IRS limits. Investment decisions belong to the employee. The recordkeeper provides beneficiary management, rollover support, fee disclosures, loans, hardship withdrawal information, and educational sessions. TicketGenie and company staff do not provide individualized tax or investment advice. The formal summary plan description controls if it differs from this overview.
