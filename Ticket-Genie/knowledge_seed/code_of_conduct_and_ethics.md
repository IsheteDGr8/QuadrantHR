# Code of Conduct, Ethics, and Speak-Up Policy

Owner: Legal and Compliance | Effective: January 1, 2026

Employees must act honestly, maintain accurate records, protect confidential information, avoid bribery and improper gifts, disclose actual or potential conflicts of interest, and treat coworkers and business partners respectfully. Company assets and systems are for legitimate business use, with limited personal use allowed when lawful, secure, and non-disruptive.

Potential misconduct may be reported to a manager, Compliance, People Operations, or the confidential ethics hotline. Anonymous reports are accepted where legally permitted. Retaliation, intimidation, or interference with a report or investigation is prohibited. Employees must preserve relevant records and cooperate truthfully with authorized investigations. Corrective action is based on substantiated facts and may include training, process changes, discipline, or termination.
