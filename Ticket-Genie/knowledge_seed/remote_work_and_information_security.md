# Remote Work and Information Security Policy

Owner: Information Security and People Operations | Effective: January 1, 2026

Approved remote employees must protect company information to the same standard as on company premises. Company work must be performed on managed devices using multifactor authentication, approved cloud services, and the corporate VPN when accessing restricted systems. Password sharing, personal cloud storage, unapproved generative-AI services, and local storage of restricted data are prohibited.

Employees must lock screens when unattended, prevent household members from using company devices, use a privacy-conscious workspace, and report suspected phishing, loss, theft, or unauthorized disclosure immediately through the security incident channel. Public Wi-Fi requires the corporate VPN; highly confidential work should be deferred when physical privacy cannot be maintained.

Remote-work approval does not change work hours, performance expectations, overtime rules, or the duty to follow safety requirements. Equipment and ergonomic requests should be submitted through TicketGenie. Security may remotely protect or wipe company data on a lost managed device.
