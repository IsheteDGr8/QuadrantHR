# Disability, Pregnancy, and Religious Accommodation Policy

Owner: People Operations | Effective: January 1, 2026

Employees and applicants may request a reasonable accommodation for disability, pregnancy or related conditions, sincerely held religious practice, or another legally protected need. No special wording or medical diagnosis is required to start the process. Requests may be made through a private HR ticket, to People Operations, or to a manager, who must forward the request promptly.

People Operations conducts an interactive process to understand essential job functions, limitations, duration, and possible accommodations. Medical information is collected only when necessary, stored separately from personnel records, and shared strictly on a need-to-know basis. Potential accommodations include schedule changes, assistive technology, modified equipment, leave, remote-work adjustments, or reassignment to a vacant role. The company will not retaliate against a person for requesting or using an accommodation.
