# Sick, Safe, Family, and Parental Leave Policy

Owner: People Operations | Effective: January 1, 2026

Employees receive 10 paid sick days per calendar year, prorated for eligible part-time schedules. Sick time may be used for the employee's illness, preventive care, mental-health needs, care of a qualifying family member, or qualifying safe-leave reasons. Employees should notify their manager before the shift when feasible, but need not disclose a diagnosis. Documentation may be requested only when permitted by law and must be sent confidentially to People Operations.

Eligible birth, adoption, or foster-placement events receive up to 12 weeks of paid parental leave, to be used within 12 months of the event. Paid company leave runs concurrently with applicable statutory leave where permitted. Employees requesting medical, family, military-caregiver, pregnancy, disability, or other protected leave should open a private HR ticket; People Operations will provide eligibility notices and an individualized designation.

Managers must not discourage leave, count protected absences under attendance programs, or retaliate against anyone who requests or uses leave. State and local entitlements that are more generous supersede these minimums.
