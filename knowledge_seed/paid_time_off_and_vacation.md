# Paid Time Off and Vacation Policy

Owner: People Operations | Effective: January 1, 2026

Full-time employees accrue 15 days of PTO during years 0–2, 20 days during years 3–5, and 25 days after five completed years. Accrual occurs each pay period and is prorated for eligible part-time schedules. Employees may carry up to 40 hours into the next calendar year unless local law requires otherwise. Balances above the cap expire after December 31 and are not converted to cash.

Planned PTO should be requested in the HR system at least 10 calendar days in advance. Managers evaluate requests based on coverage, business deadlines, and order received, and must apply the policy consistently. Employees should not make non-refundable travel commitments before approval. Emergencies may be reported as soon as practicable.

PTO is intended for rest, vacation, appointments, and personal obligations. Employees are expected to disconnect while on approved PTO. Managers may not require routine work or retaliate against an employee for using approved leave. Treatment of unused PTO at separation follows applicable state or local law. This policy does not replace protected statutory leave.
