# Equal Employment, Anti-Discrimination, and Anti-Harassment Policy

Owner: Legal and People Operations | Zero tolerance for retaliation

Northstar provides equal employment opportunity and prohibits discrimination, harassment, and retaliation based on any status protected by applicable law, including race, color, religion, creed, national origin, ancestry, sex, pregnancy, sexual orientation, gender identity or expression, age, disability, genetic information, veteran status, marital status, or any other protected characteristic.

Prohibited conduct includes slurs, threats, unwanted sexual attention, quid-pro-quo requests, hostile visual or electronic material, exclusionary conduct, and repeated unwelcome behavior that affects the workplace. The policy applies to employees, applicants, contractors, vendors, customers, remote communications, business travel, and company events.

Concerns may be reported to any manager, People Operations, the confidential ethics hotline, or the reporting employee's preferred available channel. Managers who receive a concern must promptly notify People Operations and must not investigate independently. Reports are assessed promptly, impartially, and as confidentially as practical. Retaliation for a good-faith report, participation in an investigation, requesting an accommodation, or opposing suspected discrimination is prohibited. Knowingly false reports may result in discipline, but an unsubstantiated good-faith report is not a false report.
