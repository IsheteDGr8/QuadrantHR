# Employee Benefits and Insurance Policy

Owner: People Operations | Review cycle: Annual | Effective: January 1, 2026

## Eligibility and enrollment
Regular employees scheduled for at least 30 hours per week are eligible for medical, dental, vision, basic life, accidental death and dismemberment, and short- and long-term disability coverage. Coverage begins on the first day of the month after the employee's start date. New hires must make elections within 30 calendar days. The annual open-enrollment window is announced by People Operations each fall; qualifying life events permit changes within 30 days of the event.

## Medical, dental, and vision
Northstar offers multiple medical plan designs, including a high-deductible plan compatible with a health savings account. Plan summaries, networks, payroll deductions, deductibles, and out-of-pocket limits are controlled by the current plan documents in the benefits portal. Dental coverage includes preventive care and tiered coverage for basic and major services. Vision coverage provides a routine examination and allowances for lenses, frames, or contacts according to the plan schedule.

## Continuation and claims
Employees should submit claims and coverage disputes to the applicable carrier. People Operations can explain enrollment records but cannot make clinical or claim determinations. When employment or eligibility ends, continuation notices are issued when required by applicable law. If this policy conflicts with a carrier certificate or formal plan document, the formal plan document controls.

## Privacy and support
Health information must not be included in ordinary help-desk tickets. Employees should use the confidential benefits channel for sensitive documents. Contact People Operations through a private HR ticket for enrollment corrections, dependent eligibility, or accommodation-related benefit questions.
