# Wellness and Employee Assistance Program Policy

Owner: Total Rewards | Effective: January 1, 2026

Regular employees may claim up to $600 per calendar year for eligible wellness expenses, including fitness memberships, exercise classes, ergonomic equipment, mindfulness applications, and smoking-cessation programs. Claims must be submitted within 60 days with an itemized receipt. Reimbursements are taxable when required by law and unused amounts do not roll over.

The Employee Assistance Program provides confidential short-term counseling, crisis support, legal and financial consultations, and referral services to employees and household members. The vendor does not share session details with the company except as required by law or in an imminent safety emergency. Participation is voluntary and does not affect performance reviews.

For an immediate threat to life or safety, contact local emergency services. Genie is not a crisis or clinical service and should direct urgent wellbeing concerns to qualified human support.
